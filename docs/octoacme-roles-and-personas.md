# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead owns the quality validation strategy for the project. They define test plans, coordinate testing activities across sprint cycles, and serve as the quality gate before releases. They work closely with Developers and the Technical Lead to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and non-functional requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the overall test strategy and quality standards
- Create, review, and maintain test plans and test cases
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities across the team
- Track defects, prioritize fixes, and validate resolutions
- Participate in sprint planning to assess testability and estimate QA effort
- Act as the quality gate for release readiness decisions

### Goals
- Ensure every release meets functional and non-functional quality standards
- Reduce defect escape rate to production
- Increase automated test coverage over time
- Provide early risk signals to the Project Manager and Technical Lead

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and review participation to flag quality risks
- Defect triage meetings with Developers and Technical Lead
- Release readiness reports shared with Project Manager and Product Manager
- Test result summaries and sign-off documentation prior to deployments

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead/Architect provides technical direction for the project. They guide design decisions, evaluate trade-offs, and ensure the solution is scalable, secure, and aligned with organizational standards. They bridge the gap between product requirements and implementation details, working closely with Developers, the Product Manager, and the Project Manager.

### Responsibilities
- Define and document the technical architecture and design standards
- Lead design reviews and provide guidance on complex implementation decisions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks during planning and execution
- Review and approve significant code changes and architectural decisions
- Collaborate with the Product Manager to ensure technical feasibility of requirements
- Mentor Developers on best practices and coding standards

### Goals
- Deliver a robust, maintainable, and scalable technical solution
- Minimize technical debt while meeting business timelines
- Ensure consistency and quality of technical decisions across the team
- Align technical design with long-term organizational architecture goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and design documents
- Technical design reviews with Developers and QA/Testing Lead
- Planning sessions with Project Manager to assess complexity and risk
- Regular syncs with the Product Manager on feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master/Agile Coach facilitates agile ceremonies, removes blockers, and enables the team to continuously improve its delivery process. They protect the team from external distractions and coach members on agile principles. They work across all roles to foster collaboration, transparency, and a healthy team dynamic.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies: planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and remove impediments that block team progress
- Track team velocity and help forecast delivery timelines with the Project Manager
- Coach team members and stakeholders on agile principles and practices
- Shield the team from scope creep and unplanned interruptions during sprints
- Promote a culture of continuous improvement through retrospective action items

### Goals
- Maintain a predictable, sustainable delivery cadence
- Reduce cycle time and improve team throughput over time
- Foster psychological safety and open communication within the team
- Ensure agile ceremonies are effective and add value

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies facilitation
- Retrospective notes and improvement action items shared with the team
- Blocker escalations to the Project Manager or stakeholders as needed
- Velocity and sprint health reports for planning and forecasting

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst bridges the gap between business stakeholders and the delivery team. They gather, clarify, and document requirements, ensuring that the team builds the right solution. They collaborate closely with the Product Manager, Project Manager, and Developers to translate business needs into actionable work items.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate business and functional requirements
- Facilitate requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Translate business needs into user stories, acceptance criteria, and process flows
- Maintain a shared understanding of requirements across the team and stakeholders
- Identify and document business rules, constraints, and dependencies
- Support the Product Manager in backlog refinement and prioritization

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are complete, clear, and testable before development begins
- Reduce rework caused by ambiguous or misunderstood requirements
- Strengthen alignment between stakeholder expectations and delivered solutions
- Provide a traceable link between business objectives and implemented features

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User stories and acceptance criteria documented in the project backlog
- Process flow diagrams and business rule documentation shared with Developers and QA/Testing Lead
- Regular check-ins with the Product Manager and Project Manager during planning

---

## Support/Operations Manager

### Role Summary
The Support/Operations Manager ensures the team is prepared for production deployment and that customer support processes are in place before and after a release. They represent the operational perspective throughout the project lifecycle and collaborate with the Technical Lead, Project Manager, and QA/Testing Lead to validate production readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define and validate operational readiness criteria for each release
- Coordinate runbooks, rollback procedures, and deployment checklists with the Technical Lead
- Ensure monitoring, alerting, and on-call rotations are in place before go-live
- Manage post-release support processes and triage incoming production issues
- Communicate planned maintenance windows and release impacts to customers and stakeholders
- Capture operational feedback and feed it back to the Product Manager and Project Manager for future planning

### Goals
- Ensure every release is operationally ready with minimal customer disruption
- Reduce mean time to resolution (MTTR) for production incidents
- Improve the feedback loop between production operations and the development team
- Build and maintain reliable support processes that scale with the product

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews with Project Manager, QA/Testing Lead, and Technical Lead
- Runbooks and deployment guides shared with the engineering team
- Post-release incident reports and retrospectives with the full team
- Customer communication drafts coordinated with the Product Manager
